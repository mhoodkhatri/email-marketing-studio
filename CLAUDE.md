# Email Marketing Studio

This studio is where we plan and write email campaigns for a small business. We plan each campaign, write the emails, tailor them for different groups of customers, and send them safely in practice mode.

## Folders

- `brand/`: how the brand sounds, including the voice guide and the words we use and avoid.
- `leads/`: the practice customer list we write and send to.
- `campaigns/`: one folder per campaign, holding its plan and its emails.
- `notes/`: research, decisions and working notes.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Use short sentences and everyday words.
- Never send email to real people.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
