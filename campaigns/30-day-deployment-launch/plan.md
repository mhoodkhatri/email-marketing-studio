# Campaign plan: 30-Day AI Deployment launch

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** AI, deployed in 30 days
- **Summary:** A 4-week email campaign that introduces the 30-Day AI Deployment to our opted-in list and invites founders to book a discovery call.
- **Primary goal:** **30 discovery calls booked between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.**
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Learn which of the three groups responds best, to guide the January follow-up.
  - Collect replies that tell us which business function each founder wants to fix first.
  - Grow a list of warm prospects for a January follow-up if they can't take a call before the holidays.

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 people who opted in to hear from Averon Labs. They are founders, owners and managing directors of businesses with 10 to 50 staff in the US, the UK and the Gulf. They are split into three groups:

| Group | Who | Core pain | What we'd deploy first |
|---|---|---|---|
| **real-estate** | Independent brokerages, letting agencies, small developers | Slow replies to property enquiries, leads lost after hours, agents chasing unqualified leads | Instant enquiry replies, lead qualification, viewing booking, follow-up |
| **healthcare** | Private clinics, dental, physio and aesthetics practices | Phones and inboxes full of booking requests, no-shows, a stretched front desk | Booking and rescheduling, reminders, answers to common non-clinical questions |
| **ecommerce** | D2C fashion, beauty and lifestyle brands | Support tickets rising with sales, the same order and returns questions every day | Order-status and returns answers, support triage, shopping assistant |

- **Shared motivation:** they are growing fast with a stretched team. They're open to AI but want proof before a big commitment. Many have tried an AI tool that "never really worked".
- **Buying stage:** consideration. They opted in, so they know who we are. The campaign moves them toward a decision: booking a call.
- **Where they spend time:** their inbox, LinkedIn and industry groups. This campaign is email only.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** In 30 days, we take one business function from manual work to a live AI system your team uses every day, measured against a baseline you agree with us.

**Supporting messages:**

1. **A fixed scope and a fixed timeline.** One function, 30 days, a live system. You see evidence before any larger commitment.
   *Proof:* the four phases (Discover, Build, Deploy, Prove) and the Day 30 results review.
2. **Built inside the tools you already use.** Your CRM, inbox, booking system or store. Not another app your team has to remember to open.
   *Proof:* our systems integration practice, and the day-in-the-life table in `Averon-Labs.md`.
3. **Your team stays in control.** AI handles the volume. Your people keep the decisions that matter. In clinics, anything clinical goes to staff.
   *Proof:* human oversight is one of our working principles, and every engagement ends in a handover and training.
4. **We show working products, not slide decks.** We've built and tested real systems.
   *Proof:* Doctor Booking (41 automated test files) and Style AI (live, 37 automated test files). For questions answered from a company's own documents: evaluated on a 173-question test set with published results. These are presented as **products and working demos, never as client results.**

**Variations by group:** each group's version changes the opening line and one example to match its pain (enquiries, bookings or support tickets). The structure, subject, link and footer stay the same.

**Variations by market:** Gulf readers get the more formal, warm opening described in `brand/voice.md`.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why it fits | Format | Effort | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Email (owned)** | The list has opted in. It's direct and personal, costs almost nothing, and suits founders who read on their phone between meetings. | 4 plain, short emails, each with one link to the booking page | Low to medium | Time only |
| **Booking page (owned)** | The single place every email points to | One page explaining the 30-Day AI Deployment, with a call booking form | Medium | Time only |

No paid ads, as the brief says. Social and PR are out of scope.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Holiday timing.** Weeks 3 and 4 run into Christmas and New Year, when many US and UK founders are away. | Email 4 invites readers to book a January call rather than one this week. Count calls booked for January dates toward the goal. |
| **Proof gap.** We have no client deployments yet, so readers may want case studies we can't give. | Lead with working products and verified test evidence. Offer the Day 30 results review as the proof. Never imply client results (see `brand/voice.md`). |
| **One message for three groups.** A generic email could land with nobody. | Personalize each email's opening line and example per group, and track bookings by group. |
| **Clinic sensitivity.** Healthcare readers may worry about AI giving medical advice. | Say clearly in every healthcare version that anything clinical goes to staff, and that patient data is handled with privacy first. |
