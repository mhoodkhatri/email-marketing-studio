# Averon Labs brand voice

Averon Labs is an AI engineering firm. We build and deploy AI systems inside the daily operations of growing, founder-led businesses in real estate, healthcare and e-commerce, across the US, the UK and the Gulf. Full business brief: `Averon-Labs.md`.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Direct. Practical. Accountable.**

- **Direct:** get to the point fast. Respect the reader's time. One idea per sentence.
- **Practical:** talk about the reader's real work: their inbox, bookings, leads and team. Show what changes on a normal day.
- **Accountable:** promise only what we can prove. Talk about baselines, evidence and handover, not hype.

### Tone adjustments

The core voice stays the same everywhere. Adjust it for these two audiences:

- **Gulf:** more formal and warm, and relationship-led. Open with a respectful greeting. Acknowledge the owner's time and position. Don't rush to the ask. Don't plan sends for Friday, which is a weekend or half day across much of the region. Always write in English.
- **AI product companies:** technical language is welcome. Terms like LLM, RAG, evals, agentic workflows and integration architecture are fine here. Talk to them as engineers talking to engineers.

## 2. Words we use

- deployed, in production, daily operations, live system
- forward-deployed engineers, embedded with your team
- measured against a baseline, evidence, results review
- human oversight, your team stays in control
- 30-Day AI Deployment, Digital FTEs
- built to be owned, handover

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Hype:** revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, magic, unleash, supercharge, 10x
- **Vague AI buzz:** leverage AI, AI-powered everything, synergy, disrupt
- **Pressure:** last chance, act now, limited time, don't miss out
- **Replacing people:** replace your staff, cut headcount. AI works *alongside* the team.
- **Heavy jargon for non-technical readers:** LLM, RAG, fine-tuning, agentic. These are fine in the AI-companies variant only.

## 4. Claims we must never make

1. **No client results or case studies.** We have no client deployments yet. Describe our work only as *products and working demos*, never as clients we helped.
2. **No medical advice, and never say AI makes clinical decisions.** Anything clinical is passed to clinic staff.
3. **No prices or specific costs.** Investment is shared after the discovery call.
4. **No guaranteed ROI, revenue or percentage gains.** For example, never "double your bookings" or "cut no-shows by 40%".
5. **No legal or compliance certification.** Say "designed with GDPR and UAE/Saudi data protection laws in mind". Never say "GDPR-certified" or "fully compliant".
6. **Never "fully autonomous" or "no humans needed".** Human oversight is part of every system.
7. **No inflated demo evidence.** Use only the verified facts in `Averon-Labs.md`, such as "41 automated test files" or "evaluated on a 173-question test set".

## 5. How we sign off

Every email comes from a named person, not a faceless team. End every email, for every audience, with these three lines as plain text:

Kind regards,
{{sender_name}}
Founder, Averon Labs

## 6. Spelling and style

- Use UK English spelling in every email, for every market: enquiry, personalised, organise, programme.
- Keep spelling the same across all group and market versions of an email.
- Write as "we" for the company. Keep "I" for the sender's own sign-off and personal asides only.
- Don't use contractions in email body copy (write "cannot", "it is"). The fixed footer line is the only exception.
